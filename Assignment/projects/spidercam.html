Spidercam

Technical vector illustrations of two Spidercam system views

Vector Design Illustration Technical Visual Graphic Design

Project Overview

This project focused on translating the Spidercam system into clean, detailed vector illustrations. The goal was to simplify a technical subject into visuals that are polished, readable, and accurate.

I created two distinct views to better communicate the structure and design of the system from different angles, allowing the product to be understood more clearly in a visual format.

  • Built from real product reference imagery
  • Focused on precision, clarity, and clean vector form
  • Presented multiple views to improve understanding

Field Dolly — Angled Side View

Angled side perspective showing the wider structure, mounted camera setup, and upper support system.

Spidercam X Dolly — Front View

Front-facing view highlighting the vertical frame construction and central mechanical setup.

Design Focus

My focus was on creating a visually clean technical illustration while preserving important structural details. I paid close attention to silhouette, proportion, and shape clarity so the design would remain readable even with complex parts.

What This Project Shows

This project demonstrates my ability to transform product references into organized vector artwork. It highlights attention to detail, control of shape and spacing, and the ability to present technical objects in a professional visual style.

Outcome

These two Spidercam illustrations helped me strengthen my vector illustration skills and my ability to communicate product form clearly. Showing both the angled side view and front view gives the project more depth and makes the final presentation stronger.
